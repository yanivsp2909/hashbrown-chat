## Direction contract
THESIS: A night-shift snack-bar kitchen run by a potato. It refuses the Discord-gray-plus-blurple chat default: the shell is roasted and warm, and whatever needs you hangs on a ticket rail.
OWN-WORLD: Dark roasted-brown ground with crust-brown panels and fryer-oil warm neutrals. Golden-crisp is the only action colour (buttons, links, your turn, clickable cells) and appears nowhere decorative. Ketchup red means error or loss, pickle green means tokens or money, mustard means event, and lavender means private. Light theme: parchment-free "bun" tan with the same roles. System rounded sans, with tabular figures for numbers.
STORY: You land on a ticket-shaped login card, log in, and see the room, chat and quick buttons. Challenges arrive as tickets; you press Accept and the board appears in the game panel; you win and the ticket stamps itself.
FIRST VIEWPORT (desktop 1440x900): left rail 260px (room title, balance, quick buttons, challenge form); centre chat column with the ticket rail strip on top, messages and the composer at the bottom; right panel 380px holding the game board (an empty state with an invitation). Phone: one column, with a sliding drawer for the rail/sidebar and the game panel as a sheet.
FORM: Fry Station, position 7 on my list (literal reading of the name); seed key 1346f798. Raises: from the botanical folio, every board uses one fixed plate size and baseline; from Labanotation, an invite ticket shows its exact 180 s as a draining bar; from the gate board, your-turn and unseen DMs stay lit until noticed; from the warm consumer app, gold is used only for actions.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

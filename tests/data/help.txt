📖 COMMANDS

💬 Chat
  /help                            show this list
  /users                           who's online and where
  /send name message               private message 💌
  /remind 10m text                 a private reminder later ⏰ (/remind alone: yours)
  /pin text                        pin this room's topic 📌 (/pin alone: see it, /pin clear)
  /search word                     find it in your rooms' chat 🔎
  /me action                       act it out: * Yaniv dances
  /nick newname                    change your display name
  /whois name                      someone's profile
  /quit                            leave the chat
  /poll question | a | b           ask the room to vote 📊 (/poll alone: the votes)
  /vote number                     vote in the room's poll
  /endpoll                         close your poll and show who won

👤 Profile
  /profile [name]                  someone's profile card 👤 (yours without a name)
  /setprofile field value          change your profile (avatar, bio, status...)
  /password old new                change your password (the old one first) 🔑

🤝 Friends
  /friend add name                 ask someone to be friends 🤝 (also: remove, accept, decline)
  /friends                         your friends, where they are, and who asked 🤝

👥 Groups
  /groups                          list all groups
  /create name [private]           new group (50 🪙)
  /join name                       enter a group (or ask to)
  /lobby                           go back to the lobby
  /invites [decline group]         your group invites 📨 (/join group accepts one)

🏦 Group bank
  /topgroups                       richest groups

🪙 Tokens
  /balance [name]                  how many tokens 🪙
  /top                             richest players 🏆
  /give name amount                send tokens to someone
  /daily                           free tokens every day, more for streaks 🔥
  /rob name                        40% chance to steal, or pay a fine 🦹
  /shop                            badges for your name
  /buy badge                       buy a badge from /shop (or shares: /buy FRY 5)
  /badge emoji|off                 wear a badge you own
  /achievements                    your trophies and how to get the rest

🎰 Casino
  /coinflip amount heads|tails     double or nothing
  /dice amount                     2 dice vs the house 🎲
  /slots amount                    3 same = x10, 💎💎💎 = x25
  /jackpot                         the pot that three 💎 on /slots wins 💰

📈 Market
  /stocks                          stock prices 📈 (/buy FRY 5, /sell FRY 5)
  /sell FRY 5                      sell shares (or a badge: /sell badge 🔥 300)
  /portfolio                       your shares and what they're worth
  /market [buy N | cancel N]       players' badges for sale 🛒
  /bid amount                      bid in the lobby's auction 🔨

🛠️ Admin
  /auction                         admin: start an auction of a rare badge now
  /admin password                  become a server admin
  /admintokens [name]              admin: +1000 🪙
  /hbstats                         admin: Hashbrown's questions, tokens and cost today 📊

🏦 Savings & loans
  /save amount                     put tokens in savings (interest every day) 🏦
  /withdraw amount                 take tokens out of savings
  /savings                         your savings and loan
  /loan amount                     borrow tokens (you pay back a bit more) 💳
  /repay [amount|all]              pay your loan back

🎮 Games
  /games                           what you can play
  /challenge name game [bet]       invite to a 1v1
  /accept [name]                   accept a challenge or a file 📎
  /decline [name]                  say no to a challenge or a file
  /draw                            start a drawing round: draw your word, the others guess 🎨 (/draw stop)
  /quests                          today's three small tasks, each pays 🪙 🎯
  /move ...                        a move (or just type it)
  /watch [name]                    watch someone's game 👀 (/watch alone: stop)
  /leaderboard [elo|wins|tokens]   the best players (ELO by default) 📈
  /hint                            a Wordle hint from Hashbrown, one per game (20 🪙)
  /rematch                         challenge your last opponent again 🔁
  /claim                           win if the other player hasn't moved for 2 min ⏰
  /board                           show the board again
  /resign                          give up the game 🏳️
  /wordle                          solo word game, win tokens 🟩
  /g word                          guess in your Wordle
  /giveup                          quit your Wordle

📁 Files
  /sendfile name path              send a file (up to 2 MB) 📁

🥔 Hashbrown
  /ask question                    ask Hashbrown, the AI (20 🪙)
  /tldr                            Hashbrown sums up the chat, only for you (20 🪙)
  /judge                           Hashbrown judges the argument, everyone sees it (20 🪙)
  /tr text                         translate Hebrew <-> English, only for you (20 🪙)
  /qotd                            today's question of the day ❓
  /answer text                     answer the question of the day; the most reactions wins 50 🪙
  /trivia                          Hashbrown asks the room a trivia question (20 🪙)

🌐 Network
  /ping                            how long a packet takes there and back 🏓
  /netstats                        packets and bytes on your connection 📊

👥 Open a group's chat to see its commands too (kick, mute, bank, leave...).

Anything that isn't a command is a chat message to the room you're in.
🥔 Start a message with @hashbrown to ask the AI (20 🪙).
